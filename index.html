Our mission

Care with purpose, since 2002.

Cherie's Tender Care Service, LLC is a social service agency organized in the State of Louisiana. Since April 2002, we have been dedicated to assisting people with developmental disabilities.

We provide quality attendant care services to help infants, children, and adults with developmental disabilities or mental illness achieve the greatest possible independence while maintaining the highest possible quality of life.

We believe people with disabilities should have access to a broad range of services and living options, including the opportunity to live in the least restrictive setting possible.

The people behind the care

Dedicated to helping people thrive.

Our staff is dedicated to enhancing the quality of life of every person we serve.

Professional care, a holistic approach

Our team includes professionals and paraprofessionals. We provide early intervention and support with a holistic approach that considers each person's needs and well-being.

Our staff receives training in:

  • Behavior management techniques
  • CPR and first aid
  • Seizure management
  • Medication administration
  • Medical equipment

Our services

Support for every step.

We provide personalized support through a range of long-term care, waiver, and child health services. Explore the services below and contact us to discuss your needs.

01 LT

Long-Term Care

Ongoing support for eligible individuals who need help with daily activities and care over an extended period.

Ask us about this →
02 PCA

Personal Care Services

One-to-one help with personal care and everyday activities, based on the individual's authorized care plan.

Ask us about this →
03 SIL

Supervised Independent Living

Individualized supervision and support to help people build daily living skills and participate in their community.

Ask us about this →
04 CCW

Community Choice Waiver

Community-based waiver supports for eligible individuals, helping them receive services in a community setting.

Ask us about this →
05 Children's Choice

Children's Choice Waiver

Waiver supports for eligible children with developmental disabilities, designed to help them remain with family in the community.

Ask us about this →
06 ROW

Residential Options Waiver

Individualized home- and community-based supports for eligible people with developmental disabilities.

Ask us about this →
07 EPSDT

EPSDT Services

Medicaid's child health benefit for eligible members under 21, including screenings and medically necessary services under program rules.

Ask us about this →

Services depend on eligibility, assessment, authorization, and program guidelines. Contact us to discuss availability and the support that may fit your situation.
